Proof vs. Search

Theorem proving in Lean, next to the SAT-based model checking you know from Jasper, on real RTL, ending with lowRISC's Ibex core.

  1. Follow the course in the sidebar, top to bottom. Five parts, one chapter at a time.
  2. Each chapter: read, then do. The reading is on the chapter page; its buttons open the right lab with everything selected. A bar at the top brings you back to the chapter.
  3. Tick a chapter when you are done, and go on with Next.

Every document of the course. To learn, follow the chapters in the sidebar instead.
